FAQ

Common questions answered

Nextel is a virtual telecom operator and performance advertising network with a verified audience that brands pay to reach.

Nextel connects top brands with a verified, high-value audience. It's a win-win ecosystem built on mutual growth: brands spend to reach, and our audience engages to earn!

Register for free and activate your eSIM. Stay connected, grow, and earn.

The three ad formats available on Nextel are: Sponsored Calls, Sponsored SMS, and Interactive Push Alerts.

Nextel eSIM is a unique virtual number. It serves as your membership card, your financial access pass, and your proof of belonging on the network.

There are two distinct eSIM plans: Diamond and Royal. The eSIM plan you choose determines the level of services and products you'll get.

NexPage is a free personal website that Nextel builds and launches for you the moment you activate your eSIM. You do not design it, host it, or manage it; Nextel handles all of that. It earns money for you through ad revenue and VTU commissions as Nextel drives traffic to it daily.

You can confidently make money on Nextel through Sponsored Calls, Sponsored SMS, becoming a shareholder, and having a personalized website!

You can spend your earnings using a personalized card, or withdraw via bank transfer.
